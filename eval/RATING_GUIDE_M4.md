# M4 rating guide (for all four raters)

Allow about 1 hour, including 10 minutes of calibration first. **Rate alone, and don't discuss images until you've
finished.**

## What you see
- **One sheet per scene.** On the **left** is the pose skeleton (labelled "pose"). Then come several paintings, each
  under a random code (e.g. `K7`). The codes hide which method made the image.
- **Your rows:** in the shared Google Sheet, each row is one (scene, code) for you. The first 5 rows are marked
  `calibration` and everyone rates them.

## The 4 scores (1-5; whole numbers only)
| Score | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Style** | photographic or modern-looking | painted look, but stiff or cluttered | reads as a dignified, balanced Varma-style painting |
| **Pose match** (to the skeleton on the left) | skeleton ignored | roughly the right figures and posture, limbs wrong | limbs and placement follow the skeleton |
| **Faces** | distorted or missing | smeared but recognisable | convincing as a painted face |
| **Hands** | malformed or merged | plausible blobs | convincing hands |

- **2 and 4** are in between. Use them.
- **Hands not visible** (hidden in robes, out of frame): write `NV`, not a number.
- **Faces and hands are separate.** Don't let a bad face lower the hands score, or the other way round.
- **Pose match** is only about following the skeleton. Whether the scene is right is judged elsewhere (the automatic
  checklist).

## Failure column (choose all that apply, separated by commas)
pose ignored · pose followed but scene wrong · extra/missing limbs · merged/duplicated figures · face distorted ·
hands malformed · style weakened (photographic) · stiff/over-constrained · post-processing artifacts · other

## Calibration (first 10 minutes)
1. **Rate:** everyone rates the 5 `calibration` rows alone.
2. **Compare:** compare the scores together. If two people read an anchor differently (e.g. "is a smeared face a 2
   or a 3?"), agree a wording, and B writes it into this file before anyone continues.
3. **Then rate your remaining rows alone.**

## Rules
- Don't try to guess the method from the code.
- Don't look up the key. B keeps it private until all ratings are in.
- If an image fails to load or looks broken, write `broken` in notes, leave the scores blank, and tell B.
