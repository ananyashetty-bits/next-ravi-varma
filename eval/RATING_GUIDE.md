# Rating guide (everyone reads this before rating; owner B)

You will see **contact sheets**: one scene per image, with every pipeline's picture side by side, each labelled only with a **random code** (e.g. `K4`). You don't know which pipeline made which picture. That is the point: please don't try to guess, and don't discuss ratings with anyone until everyone has submitted.

## For each (scene, code) row assigned to you, give three scores from 1 to 5

| Score | Question | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Faithfulness** | Does the picture show *this scene*: the right figures, how many, what they're doing, where? | unrelated picture | some parts right, key parts wrong or missing | everything important is there |
| **Style** | Does it look like a Raja Ravi Varma oil painting? | photo / cartoon / generic AI art | painterly but not Varma | could pass as a Varma |
| **Quality** | Anatomy, faces, hands, artifacts | broken (melted faces, extra limbs) | small flaws | clean |

Judge faithfulness against the **scene text on the sheet**, not against what you think the system could do. Judge each picture on its own; don't rank them against each other.

## Failure column: pick the ONE main problem (or leave empty if none)
`wrong figure count` · `wrong/missing figure identity` · `wrong setting` · `wrong action` · `generic figure (name ignored)` · `weak style` · `anatomy/face artifacts` · `other` (write it in notes)

## Rules
- Rate **alone**; submit before talking about it.
- Only rate rows with your letter. Each image gets at least two raters.
- About 1 hour per round. Take a break halfway; tiredness changes scores.
- If an image doesn't load, write `missing` in notes and skip it.
